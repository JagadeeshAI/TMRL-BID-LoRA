\section*{\textcolor{red}{Broader Impact Statement}}
\textcolor{red}{BID-LoRA's continual unlearning capability supports privacy compliance, such as GDPR/CCPA data-deletion requests, but is dual-use: the same enrollment/removal mechanism could extend surveillance or access-control systems, particularly given our face-recognition evaluation. Our empirical metrics, namely forget accuracy, MIA, and KL divergence, demonstrate promising unlearning behavior but do not constitute a formal privacy guarantee. Residual information may remain recoverable under stronger or unforeseen attacks beyond those evaluated here (Section~\ref{7.7}). Because face-recognition accuracy can vary across demographic groups, repeated learn-forget cycles may introduce or amplify subgroup disparities, warranting fairness audits. Finally, the retain-data replay buffer requires governance: its storage, access, and retention policies should be scrutinized to avoid becoming a new privacy risk.}
